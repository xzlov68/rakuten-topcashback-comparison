## What a Cash Back Browser Extension Actually Does

Before comparing specifics, it helps to understand the mechanics. A cash back extension is a small browser add-on that sits in your toolbar and does three things:

- **Detects cash back opportunities.** When you land on a participating retailer's site, the extension pops up an alert telling you that cash back is available and at what rate.
- **Activates tracking with one click.** Instead of navigating to a cash back portal and clicking through to the store, you just click "Activate" on the extension popup and the tracking is set.
- **Applies coupon codes automatically.** Most modern extensions also test coupon codes at checkout and apply the best working one, so you get the discount and the cash back together.

The extension doesn't change your price, doesn't redirect you anywhere sketchy, and doesn't slow down your browser. It just creates a tracking link between you, the cash back site, and the retailer so the commission gets routed back to you instead of disappearing into the portal's pocket.

The differences between extensions show up in the details: how many stores they cover, what percentage of commission they actually pass back, when and how you can withdraw, and whether they offer extras like rate comparison or a highest-rate guarantee.
